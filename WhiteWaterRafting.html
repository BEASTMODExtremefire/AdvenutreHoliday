White Water Rafting in the Swiss Alps

White-water rafting in the Swiss Alps is an exhilarating way to experience the power of nature, as you navigate fast-moving rivers surrounded by stunning alpine scenery. Whether you're rafting on the wild Lütschine River or exploring the rugged Rhine Gorge, these rivers offer a range of rapids, from gentle waves to more challenging Class IV sections, ensuring a thrilling experience for everyone.

Age Restrictions

  • Participants must be at least 12 years old (some operators allow younger children on calmer sections of the river).
  • Children and families can enjoy easier sections of the river, while adults seeking more intense rapids should opt for higher-grade routes.

More Information

  • All necessary rafting equipment, including helmets, paddles, and life vests, is provided.
  • Be sure to wear suitable clothing, such as a swimsuit or quick-dry clothes, as you will get wet. Wetsuits and water shoes are often included in your rafting gear.
  • Rafting season typically runs from May to September, depending on water levels.
  • It’s recommended to book in advance, especially in high season, as rafting tours can fill up quickly.
